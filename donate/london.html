Donating in Ontario

Where to donate used goods in London

9 organizations in and around London that take donated furniture, clothes, appliances, kitchenware and household goods. For each one: what they take, what they refuse, and whether they will come and collect it.

London's practical problem is furniture. Only two organizations on this page will come to a house for it, and only one of them — St. Vincent de Paul — will take an actual couch, on a fixed neighbourhood rota (Monday northeast, Tuesday northwest, Thursday southwest, Friday southeast). Habitat's no-cost collection is a pre-renovation salvage crew for cabinets, doors and sinks, not a moving service. Everyone else in this city refuses furniture outright: Diabetes Canada, Talize and Goodwill all name it explicitly, and Goodwill draws the line at 50 lbs. So the honest shape of a London page is: furniture goes to SSVP or it goes in a truck to TRY Recycling and costs money; clothing and small goods have four good homes; electronics that nobody wants go to the EPRA finder.

Verified 2026-09-24 from each organization's own website. Hours, fees, pickup areas and what is accepted change without notice — call or check their site before you load the car. We are not affiliated with any organization listed here and we receive nothing if you donate to them.

If you cannot move it yourself

These 5 will come to you in London. Everyone else on this page is drop-off.

  • Diabetes Canada — Declutter for Diabetes — Free home collection, but only when they have a scheduled run in your area — enter your postal code on their site to check. Items must be in sealed bags or boxes under 40 lb and left on the doorstep; loose items are not collected.
  • Habitat for Humanity Heartland Ontario ReStore — There is NO general free furniture pickup. The only no-cost collection their site describes is the Salvage & Deconstruction programme, quoted verbatim: 'Before your renovation begins, let our Salvage Team professionally remove your usable cabinets, doors, sinks, and other fixtures at no cost.' That is a pre-renovation fixture salvage, not a couch pickup. Ordinary donations go through the London donation form at https://www.habitat4home.ca/london-donation-form, where you upload photos first; the form does not state whether a pickup or a drop-off follows. Phone the store before loading a vehicle.
  • Mission Services of London — Mission Store — Their Mission Store page does describe a donation pickup request submitted through an online form, with conditions around item weight and whether the residence is accessible — this contradicts the researcher, who said no pickup exists. I read the existence of the form but not its full terms, so phone 519-433-2807 to confirm what they will collect before relying on it.
  • Recycle My Electronics Ontario (EPRA) — Their book-my-pick-up page is open to households as well as businesses but states: 'programs have minimum requirements for pick-ups. If your request doesn't meet our minimum requirements - we will be in touch to arrange a customized solution.' The minimums and any fees are not published. For a normal household load the route is drop-off, via their location finder. Their site does NOT state that drop-off is free, so do not claim it is.
  • Society of Saint Vincent de Paul London — Furniture pickup by truck, arranged in advance. Call (519) 438-7071 or email donations585york@gmail.com with photos of the furniture, the pickup address and a contact phone number. The truck runs on a neighbourhood schedule quoted on their donations page: Monday Northeast, Tuesday Northwest, Thursday Southwest, Friday Southeast. No fee is mentioned anywhere on the page, but the page never uses the word 'free' — do not badge this as free pickup. Drop-off at 585 York Street, Monday to Friday 9am-4pm.

Before you load the car

  • Phone first if it is bulky. Capacity changes week to week, and a sofa that cannot be accepted is a wasted trip for you and a wasted hour for them.
  • Clean it. Most refusals are about condition, not category. Textiles must be dry and odour-free or they get thrown out at the back door.
  • Take the parts. Bed frames need their hardware, and a dresser without its drawers is scrap to almost everyone.
  • Do not leave it outside after hours. Anything left at a closed door usually gets rained on, and the charity pays to dispose of it.
  • Ask about the receipt before you hand it over, not after.

Not sure it should be donated at all?

Some of what people give away is worth real money, and some of it will cost a charity money to dispose of. Photograph it and we will tell you which — including when the honest answer is that it is worth nothing and a thrift store is exactly the right place for it. We earn nothing when you donate.

Common questions

Will any of them pick it up from my house in London?
5 of the 9 organizations on this page collect from your home: Diabetes Canada — Declutter for Diabetes, Habitat for Humanity Heartland Ontario ReStore, Mission Services of London — Mission Store, Recycle My Electronics Ontario (EPRA), Society of Saint Vincent de Paul London. Collection usually depends on what the item is, what condition it is in, and whether you are inside their catchment. Phone first.
Do I get a tax receipt for donated goods?
Registered charities can issue a receipt for the fair market value of a gift in kind, but many thrift operations do not issue receipts for ordinary household donations because valuing them individually is not practical. Ask before you drop off if the receipt matters to you, and do not assume.
What will nobody take?
Stained or torn upholstery, mattresses in most of the country, broken particleboard furniture, exercise equipment, cribs and car seats past their expiry, and anything that has been in a fire or a flood. Most organizations list their refusals on their own site, and the list above repeats them where they do.
Is my stuff worth money instead of donating it?
Sometimes, and it is worth thirty seconds to find out. A dead car, a garage of tools or mid-century teak can be worth real money. A sofa is usually worth nothing, no matter how good it looks. We will tell you which one you have got before you decide.
