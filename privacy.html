Privacy & terms

The short version.

We set no cookies. We run no analytics and no advertising trackers. We don't know who you are unless you fill in the form, and if you do, the only things we hold are your photos, the area you typed and your email address. Ask us and we'll delete all of it.

Last updated 2 August 2026 · We Pay for Junk is an early-stage prototype operating in Mississauga, Etobicoke and Brampton, Ontario.

What we collect

Only what you type into the valuation form. Nothing else on this site collects anything about you.

WhatWhy
Photographs you uploadTo identify the item and work out what it's worth
Postal code or neighbourhoodTo know which buyers are near enough to collect
Your email addressTo send you the answer. Nothing else.
Your description, if you write oneOptional context for the valuation
The fact that you ticked consent, and whenRequired under Canadian anti-spam law

We deliberately do not ask for your name, your phone number or your street address. If you volunteer any of those in the description box, we'd rather you didn't.

What we don't do

  • No cookies. This site sets none — not for analytics, not for preferences, not at all.
  • No tracking or advertising pixels. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, nothing of that kind anywhere on the site.
  • No selling or renting your data. Not to buyers, not to marketers, not to anyone.
  • No sharing with buyers until you say so. A buyer never receives your email address unless you reply and specifically tell us to put you in touch with that buyer.
  • No training on your photos without asking you first and separately.

Who else sees anything

Being straight about this, because most privacy policies aren't. To load the page at all, your browser fetches fonts, icons and images from other companies' servers, and those companies can see your IP address as a result. We don't control that and we don't receive anything from it.

  • Netlify — hosts the site and receives, stores and emails us the form submissions. Their privacy terms apply to that data.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare — serve the typefaces.
  • unpkg and Cloudflare CDN — serve open-source JavaScript libraries.
  • Unsplash and Flickr — serve some of the photography.

How long we keep it

Form submissions are kept while we're actively working on your valuation and for up to twelve months afterwards, so we can improve how accurately we price things. Consent records are kept longer because anti-spam law requires it. Everything else goes.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA, Canada's federal privacy law, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted — and we'll do it, without asking why. There is no account to close and no dark pattern to click through. Just email us.

To reach us about any of this: privacy@wepayforjunk.com

If you'd rather not email, reply to whatever we sent you and say "delete my data". That works too.

Anti-spam (CASL)

We only email you if you ticked the consent box, and only about the item you sent us. We don't run a mailing list, and there is nothing to unsubscribe from — but if you ask us to stop contacting you, we stop, permanently and immediately.

Terms, in plain English

Our valuations are estimates, not appraisals. We tell you what things like yours have recently sold for. We are not licensed appraisers, the number is not a guarantee, and nobody is obliged to pay it. Don't rely on it for insurance, probate or tax purposes without getting a real appraisal.

We are not a party to your sale. If you sell something to a buyer you found here, that transaction is between the two of you. We don't take payment, don't hold funds, don't deliver anything and don't guarantee that either side behaves well. Use ordinary sense: meet safely, don't let strangers into your home alone, and count the money.

Much of this site is still a demonstration. The listings, buyers, valuations and activity shown on the site are sample data built to show how the product works. The valuation form is the one part that is real and reaches an actual person.

It's free, and it stays free for you. We never charge people who are getting rid of things. Commercial buyers pay us to reach sellers.
